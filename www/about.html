About RAJKOV

Who we are, how the Kutumb began, what we set out to do — and every committee that has served since 2003.

Our history

From seven families to a community

Rajasthani Kutumb of Victoria was formed in 2001 in Melbourne by seven Rajasthani families. The main focus was to celebrate Rajasthani festivals — Teej and Gangaur specifically — in Australia in the traditional Rajasthani manner.

The Kutumb was incorporated in 2003. All six paid life members at that time became the founder members of the organisation: the Jawale, Goyal, Manihar, Panwar, Sharma and Soni families.

Since then RAJKOV has grown from a festival circle into a broad community association — with initiatives for young people, business, spirituality, sport and members overseas, and partnerships with Rajasthani organisations around the world.

Statement of purposes

What the Kutumb exists to do

From rule 3 of our constitution, the association aims:

  1. To sustain and promote Rajasthani culture — including Rajasthani languages, literature, art and craft, Rajasthani food, heritage, festivals and dress — and social interests.
  2. To bring together the people of Rajasthani origin and to organise functions to celebrate the festivals of Rajasthan, especially Gangaur and Teej.
  3. To maintain and develop relationships with other organisations in Australia or overseas that have similar interests.
  4. To acquire property of its own to host its functions and for its office purposes.

The Kutumb is a Victorian incorporated association (Reg. No. A 0044973G, ABN 91 393 557 191). Its financial and membership year runs from 1 May to 30 April. See Our Constitution, Our By-Laws and Membership.
