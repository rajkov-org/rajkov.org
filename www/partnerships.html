Our partnerships

Memoranda of Understanding with Rajasthani organisations around the world, and the sponsors who back our programme.

Why we partner

One Rajasthani family, many countries

One of the Kutumb's stated purposes is to maintain and develop relationships with organisations in Australia and overseas that share our interests. Each MoU below commits both sides to promoting Rajasthani culture, supporting language and cultural education, and sharing networks and resources.
