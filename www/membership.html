Membership

Join the Kutumb — categories, eligibility, fees and how to apply.

Categories

Three ways to belong

Membership runs with the Kutumb's financial year, 1 May to 30 April. The categories are set out in rule 4 of the constitution.

Annual Family Membership

For a family with unmarried or married children and parents living under the same roof. Valid for one membership year.

Annual Single / Student / Pensioner

For singles, students and pensioners. Valid for one membership year.

Life Membership

Valid for the life of the association. Available after two years as a member; the waiting period does not apply to children of existing life members who take a separate life membership.

Fees & eligibility

What to expect

  • Joining fee — equal to the annual subscription for Family and Students/Seniors members; non-refundable and payable on top of the first year's subscription. It is also payable if a lapsed membership is renewed, and is waived for children of existing paid members who take a separate membership.
  • Annual subscription — payable in advance on or before 1 May each year. The current fee schedule is available from the committee.
  • Approval — applications go to the Secretary and are approved by the committee; once the fees are paid your name is entered in the register of members.
  • Friends of Rajasthani culture — people who are not of Rajasthani origin but show active interest in Rajasthani culture may be admitted, up to 3% of the previous year's paid membership (see the By-Laws).

Full details: Constitution, rule 4 and By-Laws — membership fees.

How to apply

Three simple steps

Step 1

Send an enquiry

Fill in the membership enquiry form (a Microsoft Form — takes two minutes).

Step 2

Hear from the committee

The Secretary refers your application to the committee, which approves it and confirms the fees payable.

Step 3

Pay and join in

Pay the joining fee and first year's subscription within 28 days, and you are on the register — welcome to the Kutumb!
