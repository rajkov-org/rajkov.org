Announcement

Dear RAJKOV Family,

Convener: Shri Neelesh Mehta

We're thrilled to announce that Shri Neelesh Mehta Ji has joined us as the Convener of the Rajkov Business Network (RBN)!

From being a serial entrepreneur, running a family office, and with a wealth of experience in capital markets and financial services across South-East Asia and Australia, Neelesh Ji's expertise spans a remarkable range of domains, from mergers and acquisitions to corporate governance and strategic financial management.

Under his stewardship, we're set to chart new heights in networking, mentorship, and business growth within our community. Neelesh Ji's appointment marks a significant step towards achieving RBN's goals of fostering professional connections, facilitating collaborative opportunities, and contributing to the prosperity of our Rajasthani community in Victoria.

Let's extend our warmest welcome to Neelesh Ji and gear up for an exciting journey of business excellence with RBN!

Aapri Sewa Mein,
The RAJKOV Committee
