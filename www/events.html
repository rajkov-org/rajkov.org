Our festivals

Gangaur and Teej, the Rajasthani way

Gangaur, in March–April, honours Gauri (Parvati) and is Rajasthan's festival of spring, marital happiness and harvest — celebrated by RAJKOV with ghoomar, folk music, traditional dress and a feast.

Teej, in the monsoon month of Shravan, welcomes the rains with swings, mehndi, songs and sweets. Both festivals were the reason seven families started the Kutumb in 2001, and they remain the heart of our year.

Members are the first to hear about dates, tickets and stalls. Become a member or follow us on social media.
