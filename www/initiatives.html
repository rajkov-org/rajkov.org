RAJKOV Spirituality Forum

Convener: Shri Ranjeet Verma

A forum to integrate a new dimension of spirituality into our Rajasthani community in Victoria.

Ram Ram Sa,

In an endeavour to integrate a new dimension of spirituality into our Rajasthani community here in Victoria, we are thrilled to announce the launch of the Rajkov Spirituality Forum.

We are pleased to appoint Shri Ranjeet Verma Sa as the Convener of this Rajkov Spirituality Forum.

Ranjeet Sa has expressed a strong desire to contribute to our Rajkov's spiritual growth, and we believe his leadership will be invaluable.

— The RAJKOV Committee

Initiative

RAJKOV Global

Convener: Shri Vishal Mehta (Florida, USA) · Co-Convener Shri Nitesh Vyas (Seattle, USA)

Keeping past and present RAJKOV members who now live outside Australia connected with, and contributing to, the community.

Ram Ram Sa,

We are delighted to announce that Shri Vishal Mehta Sa, our esteemed ex-President, has been appointed as the Convener of the newly created Rajkov Global initiative. Vishal Sa, currently based in Florida, USA, brings a wealth of experience and dedication to our community.

We are also pleased to announce that Shri Nitesh Vyas Sa, another esteemed ex-President of Rajkov and currently based in Seattle, USA, will serve as the Co-Convener of Rajkov Global. Together, both will lead this important initiative, ensuring that our global members remain connected and engaged.

Rajkov Global has been established to facilitate engagement among past and present Rajkov members who are living outside Australia. This initiative aims to create an avenue for them to support our community, fostering connections and collaboration on a global scale. Key areas where Rajkov Global will focus its efforts include:

  • Building a network of RAJKOV members worldwide.
  • Creating opportunities for members to contribute to RAJKOV's growth and initiatives.
  • Facilitating support for RAJKOV's projects and programs from our international community.

Please join us in congratulating Vishal Mehta Sa and Nitesh Vyas Sa on their new roles. We look forward to the exciting developments and contributions that RAJKOV Global will bring to our community.

Initiative

RAJKOV Stars

Our dedicated platform for nurturing the young talents of our community — fostering creativity, confidence and collaboration among young Rajkovians through extracurricular activities.

  • Debate — encouraging critical thinking and public speaking skills through structured debates on various topics.
  • Drama — offering opportunities for self-expression and performance through theatrical activities and productions.
  • Music — cultivating musical talents with classes, workshops and performances in vocal and instrumental music.
  • Art — inspiring creativity through painting, drawing and other visual arts activities.
  • Other activities — a platform for the many other activities that help the holistic development of our young members.

We believe in the power of community and the importance of nurturing young talents. Join us in our mission to create a vibrant and supportive environment for the next generation of Rajkovians.

To get involved or to join the RAJKOV Stars WhatsApp group, email the committee at secretary@rajkov.org.

Initiative

RAJKOV Mel Milaap

Convener: Smt Chanchal Kumavat (former President)

Micro-communities within RAJKOV, where member families host small events so new and current members can bond and form meaningful connections.

Ram Ram Sa Rajkov Parivar,

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our new initiative, Rajkov MelMilap! This initiative aims to foster micro communities within Rajkov, allowing members to bond and form meaningful connections.

New members can join the MelMilap group, where micro family events will be hosted by member families, enabling new and current members to engage and interact. These events will be on a cost-sharing basis among participants.

We are delighted to appoint Smt Chanchal Kumavat Sa, ex-president of Rajkov, as the Convener of Rajkov MelMilap. Chanchal ji has been instrumental in hosting similar events, and her invaluable experience will greatly benefit Rajkov.

Aapri Sewa Mein — Team RAJKOV

To join the Mel Milaap group, email the committee at secretary@rajkov.org.

Initiative

RAJKOV Cricket & Recreation

Convener: Dr Amit Singh

The RAJKOV Cricket and Recreation Club — cricket, camaraderie and an active community.

We're excited to announce Dr. Amit Singh Sa as the newly appointed Convener of our Rajkov Cricket and Recreation Club!

Dr. Amit's journey began in Delhi, India, where after completing his MBBS he served in the department of surgery at prestigious hospitals. His quest for growth led him to Australia in 2008, where he has since enriched his medical career with numerous diplomas, a fellowship in General Practice, and a specialisation in skin cancer management.

Not only is he an accomplished doctor with interests ranging from chronic disease management to minor surgical procedures, but Dr. Amit is also a passionate cricketer. He has an impressive cricketing background, having played for the Croydon Ranges, Heatherton, and even representing Delhi in the under-16 and under-19 categories. Plus, his participation in the Bangkok International Cricket Sixes showcases his dedication to the sport.

Dr. Amit's love for the game and his down-to-earth personality make him the perfect fit for leading our club. His experience and leadership are poised to ignite passion within our community and drive us forward in both cricket and camaraderie.

— The RAJKOV Committee
