## RAJKOV Spirituality Forum 

 Rajkov Spirituality Forum Announcement

# Ram Ram Sa,

In an endeavor to integrate a new dimension of spirituality into our Rajasthani community here in Victoria, we are thrilled to announce the launch of the Rajkov Spirituality Forum.

We are pleased to appoint Shri. Ranjeet Verma Sa as the Convener of this Rajkov Spirituality Forum.

Ranjeet Sa has expressed a strong desire to contribute to our Rajkov’s spiritual growth, and we believe his leadership will be invaluable.
