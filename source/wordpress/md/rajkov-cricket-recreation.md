## RAJKOV Cricket & Recreation 

 Rajkov Cricket and Recreation Club Announcement

We’re excited to announce Dr. Amit Singh Sa as the newly appointed Convener of our Rajkov Cricket and Recreation Club!

Dr. Amit’s journey began in Delhi, India, where after completing his MBBS, he served in the department of surgery at prestigious hospitals. His quest for growth led him to Australia in 2008, where he has since enriched his medical career with numerous diplomas, a fellowship in General Practice, and a specialization in skin cancer management.

Not only is he an accomplished doctor with interests ranging from chronic disease management to minor surgical procedures, but Dr. Amit is also a passionate cricketer. He has an impressive cricketing background, having played for the Croydon Ranges, Heatherton, and even representing Delhi in the under 16 and under 19 categories. Plus, his participation in the Bangkok International Cricket Sixes showcases his dedication to the sport.

Dr. Amit’s love for the game and his down-to-earth personality make him the perfect fit for leading our club. His experience and leadership are poised to ignite passion within our community and drive us forward in both cricket and camaraderie.
