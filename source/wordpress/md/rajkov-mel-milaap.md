## RAJKOV Mel Milaap 

 Rajkov MelMilap Initiative Announcement

# Ram Ram Sa Rajkov Parivar,

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our new initiative, Rajkov MelMilap!

This initiative aims to foster micro communities within Rajkov, allowing members to bond and form meaningful connections.

New members can join the MelMilap group, where micro family events will be hosted by member families, enabling new and current members to engage and interact. These events will be on a cost-sharing basis among participants.

We are delighted to appoint Smt Chanchal Kumavat Sa, ex-president of Rajkov, as the Convener of Rajkov MelMilap.

Chanchal ji has been instrumental in hosting similar events, and her invaluable experience will greatly benefit Rajkov.
